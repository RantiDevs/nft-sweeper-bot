EMAIL RULES
1. Subject line: under 7 words, specific to the business, no hype, no capital letter shouting.
2. Length: 90 to 120 words in the body.
3. Open with one sentence showing you looked at their actual site, naming something specific and genuinely good if there is one, then the single most important problem you found.
4. Mention at most 2 problems, each tied to what it costs them (missed bookings, lost enquiries, weaker trust).
5. Make one clear offer, either a free short mockup of an improved homepage or a 15 minute call. Do not offer both.
6. Close with one simple question that is easy to answer.
7. Tone: formal, warm, direct, written like a person, not a template.
8. Do not use dashes of any kind. Do not use hyphens as punctuation, em dashes, or en dashes. Use commas, full stops, or new sentences instead.
9. Do not use emoji, exclamation marks, or phrases such as "I hope this email finds you well", "I came across your website", "game changer", "cutting edge", "take your business to the next level", or "in today's digital world".
10. Do not claim results, awards, clients, or prices we have not given you. Do not promise rankings or revenue.
11. Use the owner's name if it appears in the site data. Otherwise write "Hello," followed by the business name context.
12. End with the sender signature and this line: "If you would rather not hear from me again, reply with the word stop and I will not contact you."
